This policy covers Aura Remote / Aura TV Remote for Android (package com.aistudio.auraremote.vqxmpz), a local-network television remote.

TV discovery, pairing and control

Aura discovers compatible televisions on your local network. At your request it sends pairing information, remote-button requests, app or input requests, and the text or search terms you choose to send directly to the selected television. Network discovery uses your device's local network address and responses from devices on that network. TV manufacturers, television apps and streaming services may process information they receive under their own policies.

Information stored in the app

Saved TV names, room labels, local network addresses, pairing credentials and preferences are stored in Android's private app storage. Pairing data is also exchanged with the selected TV as needed to authorize control. Recent command history is kept in session memory. Android backup is disabled for Aura. AILoopLabs does not operate a server that receives these TV profiles, pairing credentials, commands or searches.

Optional microphone and speech recognition

Voice input requires your action and microphone permission. Aura prefers on-device recognition when available. Otherwise, your Android speech-recognition provider may send audio to its servers and process related recognition information under its own privacy policy and settings. Aura explains this before voice input starts. AILoopLabs does not receive the audio. You can use remote buttons and typed input without microphone access. Disable microphone permission in Android settings at any time.

Network security

Aura uses each TV's supported local-control protocol. Some televisions use unencrypted HTTP or WebSocket connections on your local network; others use TLS. Aura does not claim that all TV traffic is encrypted. Use a trusted private network. Network access and TV authorization are required for remote control; multicast permission supports discovery. Vibration supports button feedback.

Accounts, advertising and payments

This version has no Aura account registration, advertising SDK, analytics service, in-app purchases or developer-operated cloud service. AILoopLabs does not receive microphone audio, clipboard content or TV-control history through the app. If you email us for support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to handle your request.

Retention and deletion

Remove a TV in Aura to delete its saved profile and pairing credentials from the app. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove remaining local app data. You may revoke Aura's authorization in the television's settings. Your TV and speech provider control any information they separately retain; use their settings or contact them for deletion. To request deletion of support correspondence held by AILoopLabs, email the contact above.

Audience and affiliations

Aura is a general utility for people who manage their own TVs and networks, not a service directed to children. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by television manufacturers or streaming services. Supported controls vary by TV model and protocol. Native Android/Google TV remote pairing is not available in this version.

Changes and contact

Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and any required app disclosure or consent. For privacy questions contact ailooplabs.pro@gmail.com.
