GlintBeam TV Remote - Full Manual
English narration and captions. Legacy Android 1.0.2 example states; no physical TV recording.

00:00:00.000  Meet GlintBeam
Start with your phone and television on the same trusted Wi-Fi network. Turn the television on. GlintBeam is a product by A. I Looplabs. This guide uses labeled example TV data.

00:00:13.967  Before you connect
Read the first-use tips and select Next Tip to continue. These screenshots show example interface states, not a recording of a physical television responding. Supported controls depend on the TV model.

00:00:28.567  Learn the navigation modes
GlintBeam offers a directional pad and a swipe touchpad. The setup tips introduce both modes. Later, you can switch between them on the Remote screen and use whichever feels more comfortable.

00:00:42.167  Search, apps and voice
The next tip introduces TV app shortcuts, typed searches, and optional voice input. GlintBeam sends requests to your selected TV. Streaming services may require their own accounts, payments, or subscriptions.

00:00:58.167  Begin TV discovery
Choose Search for TVs. Keep the phone and TV on the same local network. You can revisit the previous tip with Back. The tutorial checkbox controls whether these tips appear again.

00:01:11.100  If no television appears
If discovery finds nothing, confirm the TV is on and connected to the same Wi-Fi. Try Rescan Wi-Fi. Guest networks or network isolation can prevent devices from finding each other.

00:01:25.733  Choose the correct TV
Select your television from the discovered list, then use the blue pairing button. Demo TV is an example. Confirm the name and brand belong to the television you intend to control.

00:01:38.867  Connect using an IP address
If discovery misses your TV, use the manual connection section. Choose its brand and enter the TV address shown in its network settings. Do not enter an address copied from this example.

00:01:52.333  Approve on the television
Some TVs, including Samsung, show an approval request on the television. Allow GlintBeam there and wait for confirmation. This is an illustrated app state; your actual television prompt may look different.

00:02:07.533  Enter the TV’s own code
Other televisions request a PIN or configured key. Enter the value from your television or its authorized settings, then verify. Never assume the sample code in a tutorial will pair a real TV.

00:02:22.867  Handle a rejected pairing
If authorization fails, the pairing screen stays open. Check the TV, request a new prompt, or go back and select the device again. A failed request does not mean the TV is connected.

00:02:36.800  Give your TV a useful name
After the television confirms pairing, choose a recognizable name, such as Living Room TV. Assign a room so multiple televisions are easier to find. The saved profile remains in GlintBeam on this phone.

00:02:52.467  Save and open the remote
Choose Save and Open Remote after pairing and naming your television. The app then opens the main remote. The example shown here illustrates the destination; it is not evidence of hardware compatibility.

00:03:07.433  Use the directional pad
Tap the directional arrows to move through the television interface. Tap OK to select. The top row includes power, source, the number pad, and keyboard or voice access. Check the TV for each result.

00:03:22.300  Switch to swipe navigation
Choose Swipe Touchpad. Swipe to navigate, tap to select, and double-tap to go back. These gestures send navigation requests; smooth pointer movement and other behavior depend on the selected TV protocol.

00:03:37.533  Find Back, Home and volume
Scroll down the remote to reach Back, Home, Movies, Guide, Menu, volume, mute, and channel controls. Some controls depend on your TV. Displayed information is not a continuously synchronized TV status feed.

00:03:53.900  Open Movie Center
Use the bottom Movie Center tab for TV search and playback requests. Type a movie, show, or actor and send the search to your TV. GlintBeam does not supply movies or guarantee automatic playback.

00:04:08.867  Request apps and inputs
Scroll within Movie Center to find app and input shortcuts. A request works only where the selected TV protocol supports it. An app tile does not include a streaming subscription or guarantee the service is installed.

00:04:24.533  Understand input selection
Input controls vary by television. Some models can select an input directly; others can only open the TV source menu. Check the actual TV screen and make any remaining selection there.

00:04:39.067  Use the TV keyboard
Open the microphone or keyboard button on the remote. Type a search or other TV text, then send it. Use ordinary non-sensitive text: some television control protocols use unencrypted local connections.

00:04:54.700  Choose whether to use voice
Voice input is optional. Read the microphone explanation before continuing. Your Android speech provider may process audio on its servers. You can choose typed input instead; basic remote buttons do not need microphone access.

00:05:11.600  Open numbers and color keys
The dial-pad button opens channel numbers and color keys. Use these only where your TV supports them. Close the sheet to return to the remote. Sending a number does not guarantee a particular channel exists.

00:05:26.633  Manage saved televisions
Open TVs and Setup to see saved devices and rooms. Select a TV to make it active. The heart marks favorites. Removing a TV deletes its saved profile and pairing credentials from GlintBeam.

00:05:41.633  Switch devices and rooms
With more than one saved TV, use the top device selector or room filters to find the right one. Always check which TV is active before sending power, volume, search, or other commands.

00:05:55.833  Add another television
Choose Add TV to open the four-step wizard. Select the brand and read its preparation checklist. Prepare the television’s network-control settings before scanning. The wizard is separate from the initial tutorial.

00:06:11.400  Discover an additional TV
The wizard shows discovered devices for the selected brand. Select the intended television, or enter its network address manually. Example names and addresses shown in this guide must be replaced with your own TV details.

00:06:27.833  Verify authorization
Follow the authorization instructions for the selected television. Allow its prompt or enter its required code. Wait for a verified result before proceeding. Do not treat a pending handshake as successful pairing.

00:06:43.300  Check compatibility and save
In the compatibility step, try the displayed test buttons and check the television for the result. Name the device and assign its room. Only save once the app’s required verification steps are satisfied.

00:06:57.900  Read the Brand Guide
The Brand Guide tab contains preparation instructions and protocol-specific information. Select your manufacturer. Use your television’s own settings and documentation where menu names differ. This guide does not certify every model from a brand.

00:07:15.733  LG authorization
For LG, follow the TV’s registration or pairing prompt and wait for authorization. Features vary by model and supported protocol. A network connection alone is not proof that GlintBeam has permission to control the TV.

00:07:32.633  Sony IP Control
Sony uses its separate IP Control path, with the TV’s PIN or configured pre-shared key where required. This is different from native Android TV remote pairing. Use only settings and televisions you are authorized to manage.

00:07:49.933  Vizio pairing
For Vizio, use the pairing information requested by the television and wait for authentication. Check each operation on the TV screen. Do not assume every app, input, or button is available on every model.

00:08:05.300  Roku connection
Roku uses its local network-control interface and does not require an invented pairing PIN. Enable network control where the TV requires it. GlintBeam still needs to verify the device before treating the connection as ready.

00:08:22.500  Android TV limitation
Native Android and Google TV remote pairing is not available in this GlintBeam version. The presence of a brand-guide entry does not make that protocol supported. Some Sony TVs may use the separate Sony IP Control path.

00:08:39.267  Review the preparation checklist
Scroll through the selected Brand Guide for its preparation checklist and capability notes. If a connection fails, review these settings first. Only change television settings you understand and have permission to change.

00:08:54.400  Find privacy information
Privacy and Info is available at the top of the app, including during setup. Read how local profiles, TV requests, optional speech processing, and support messages are handled. Microphone audio is not received by A. I Looplabs.

00:09:11.900  Read usage information
The Usage tab explains network risks, TV compatibility, and third-party charges. GlintBeam itself has no account, advertising, or in-app purchases in this version. Television services may still have their own charges and terms.

00:09:29.100  Get help and continue
The Licenses tab lists software and font notices. For help, use the support link on the GlintBeam website. You can replay any chapter, read the written guide, or return to the quick start whenever you need it.
