Aura TV Remote - Quick Start
English narration and captions. Example app states; no physical TV recording.

00:00:00.000  Start on the same Wi-Fi
Welcome to Aura Remote, a product by AILoopLabs. Turn on your TV and connect your phone to the same trusted Wi-Fi network.

00:00:10.800  Search for your television
Read the setup tips, then choose Search for TVs. This walkthrough uses example TV data. Follow the prompts for your own television.

00:00:21.600  Select your TV
Choose your TV from the list and start its connection process. Check the name and brand. Demo TV is only an example.

00:00:32.400  Follow the TV’s prompt
Depending on the brand, approve a TV prompt or enter its required code. Roku does not need a PIN. Wait for Aura to confirm authorization.

00:00:43.933  Name, assign and save
After pairing is confirmed, name the TV, choose a room, and select Save and Open Remote. Your saved profile is stored on this phone.

00:00:54.733  Navigate with arrows and OK
Use the directional arrows to move and OK to select. Scroll for Back, Home, volume and channel controls. Check the television for each result.

00:01:05.867  Try the touchpad
Select Swipe Touchpad to use gestures. Swipe to navigate, tap to select, and double-tap to go back. Supported behavior depends on the TV.

00:01:16.667  Explore TV shortcuts
Movie Center contains search, playback, app, and input requests. Aura does not supply streaming content. Availability depends on your TV and its services.

00:01:28.600  Type or choose voice
Open the keyboard or microphone button to send text. Voice is optional. Read its explanation before allowing microphone access; typed input is also available.

00:01:41.000  Find your saved TVs
Use TVs and Setup to switch televisions, filter rooms, add devices, or remove a saved TV. Removing a device also clears its saved pairing credentials.

00:01:53.767  Use the Brand Guide
Open Brand Guide for setup instructions. Native Android and Google TV pairing is not supported in this version. Controls vary by model and protocol.

00:02:05.767  Help is always nearby
Use Privacy and Info for disclosures and notices. Visit the Aura website for the complete manual and support. You are ready to try the basic remote controls.
