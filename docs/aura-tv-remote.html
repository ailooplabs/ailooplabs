Your first connection

Start with the same Wi-Fi.

Keep your original TV remote nearby for any permission prompts. The GlintBeam guide has English narration, captions and a transcript. It shows earlier Aura app screens, so current GlintBeam screens may differ.

  1. Turn on your TV

    Connect your Android phone and TV to the same trusted private network. Guest networks may prevent devices from finding each other.

  2. Find and select the right TV

    Start discovery in GlintBeam. Check the TV’s identity before selecting it. If discovery fails, follow the guide’s manual connection steps.

  3. Authorize on the TV

    Follow the instructions for your TV brand. Approve access or enter a code only when that TV’s pairing flow asks for it.

  4. Name, save and check a control

    Choose a name and room, open the remote, and try a simple navigation command. Confirm the result on the TV itself.

Five brand protocols.
Every model is different.

GlintBeam implements local-control protocols for the following brands. This is protocol support, not a promise that every model or every command will work.

  • Samsung
  • LG
  • Sony
  • Vizio
  • Roku

Pairing, power, volume, text input, gestures, app launches and search behavior vary by TV model, firmware and protocol. The TV may require local remote control to be enabled in its settings.

Native Android/Google TV pairing is unsupported in this version. A brand name alone does not establish compatibility; check the TV platform and GlintBeam’s Brand Guide.

Always check the result on your TV. Streaming apps and TV providers may require their own accounts, subscriptions or payments. GlintBeam does not provide content and is not affiliated with or endorsed by these brands or services.

A few useful answers

Before you pick up the remote.

Is GlintBeam free? Do I need an account?

GlintBeam is free, with no GlintBeam account, no GlintBeam ads and no in-app purchases. Your TV manufacturer or streaming provider may require a separate account or payment for its services and content.

Where can I get GlintBeam?

Check Google Play listing for release status. Release submitted — public listing pending. These pages document the Android app.

Will it work with my TV?

GlintBeam implements Samsung, LG, Sony, Vizio and Roku local-control protocols. Support varies by model, firmware, settings and feature. Native Android/Google TV pairing is unsupported. Use the Brand Guide and test the requested action on your own TV.

Why can’t GlintBeam find or control my TV?

Check that the TV is on, both devices are on the same private network, and local remote control is enabled on the TV where needed. Guest network isolation can block discovery. Follow the TV’s authorization instructions and see the troubleshooting guide.

Does voice input send audio to a server?

Voice is optional and requires your action and microphone permission. GlintBeam prefers on-device recognition when available; otherwise your Android speech provider may process audio on its servers. AILoopLabs does not receive the audio. You can use buttons and typed input without microphone access. See the GlintBeam privacy policy.

How does GlintBeam handle my TV information?

Saved TV profiles and pairing credentials stay in Android’s private app storage. AILoopLabs does not operate a server that receives TV profiles, pairing credentials, commands or searches. TV protocol security varies, and some TV connections are unencrypted. Use a trusted private network and read the product-specific privacy policy.

Do the screenshots and videos show a tested TV connection?

They show actual Aura UI in labeled example app states. TV-side prompts are illustrative. Discovery results, saved profiles and on-screen command feedback do not establish a successful physical-TV connection. Check pairing and command results on your actual TV.
