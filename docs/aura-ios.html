Aura TV Remote · iPhone and iPad

Setup and support

Control a compatible Sony Bravia TV over your trusted home network. Aura for iOS is in development and is not yet available on the App Store. Roku support is planned for a later release after physical-device testing.

Get connected

  1. Turn on the Sony Bravia and connect the iPhone or iPad to the same home network. A TV connected to the same router by Ethernet can work too.
  2. Allow Local Network access when iOS asks. In Aura, search for the TV; if it is not found, enter its private IP address.
  3. Enable IP control with Normal authentication on the TV. Request a registration code in Aura and enter the four digits shown on the TV.
  4. Name the TV, open the remote, and confirm a button's effect on the TV screen.

When a TV is not found

Check that the TV is on, the phone and TV are on the same private network, and guest-network isolation is off. Confirm the TV's current IP address in its network settings. If the TV is connected by Ethernet, it must still be on the same local network as the phone.

When pairing or buttons fail

Keep the TV on its home screen during registration. If a Sony prompt closes, request a fresh code and enter it while the prompt remains visible. Check IP control settings and the TV's own approval message. Restart Aura or the TV after a network change. Some buttons, app shortcuts, and input actions vary by model and installed TV apps; a request response does not guarantee a visible action.

Privacy and security

Aura stores TV names and local IP addresses on the device. Sony credentials are stored in Keychain. Some TV protocols use unencrypted HTTP; use a trusted private network. Read the Aura for iOS privacy policy. Remove a saved TV in Aura before uninstalling if you want its Sony credential cleared.

Contact support

Email support@ailooplabs.com with your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, TV brand and model, and the step that failed. Do not send pairing codes, passwords, or TV credentials.
