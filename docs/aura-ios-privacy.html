This policy covers the iPhone and iPad version of Aura TV Remote. The Android version has a separate policy.

Local network use

Aura uses your local Wi-Fi network to find and control supported Sony Bravia and Roku devices. At your request, it sends discovery, registration, button, and app-launch requests directly to a selected TV or Roku device. The app uses your device's local IP address to look for TVs on the same network. TV makers and streaming services may process information they receive under their own policies.

Information stored on your device

The app stores TV names, room labels, local IP addresses, and app preferences in its private storage on the iPhone or iPad. Sony registration cookies are stored in the device Keychain. AILOOPLABS LLC does not operate a server that receives these TV profiles, pairing credentials, or remote-control requests.

Permissions and network security

The app asks for local-network access to find and control TVs. It does not request microphone, speech-recognition, location, or contact permissions. Some TV control protocols use unencrypted HTTP on your local network. Use Aura on a trusted private Wi-Fi network.

Accounts and analytics

The inspected iOS app has no Aura account, advertising SDK, analytics service, or in-app purchases. If you contact support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond to your request. Please do not send pairing codes or TV credentials.

Retention and deletion

Remove a TV in Aura to remove its saved profile and Sony credential from the app. Before uninstalling Aura, remove saved TVs to clear their Sony Keychain credentials; uninstalling removes app-private preferences but may leave Keychain items on the device. You can also revoke device authorization in your TV's settings. To request deletion of support correspondence held by AILoopLabs, email the contact address above.

Compatibility and changes

Compatibility varies by TV model and its local-control settings. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sony, Roku, or streaming services. Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and any required app disclosure or consent.
